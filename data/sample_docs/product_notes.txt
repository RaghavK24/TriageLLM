FROBOZZ QUANTUM WIDGET — RELEASE NOTES

v3.2.0 (released 2026-02-14)
- Added support for the new Photon Lattice API.
- Reduced cold-start latency by 42% via lazy initialization of the
  entanglement cache.
- Deprecated the legacy /widget/v1 endpoint; it will be removed in v4.0.

v3.1.0 (released 2025-11-20)
- Introduced tiered pricing: Bronze (up to 10k widgets/mo),
  Silver (up to 100k), and Gold (unlimited).
- Fixed a bug where widgets with zero mass would loop indefinitely
  during initialization.

Known issues
- The Photon Lattice API returns HTTP 429 more aggressively than
  documented. Recommend exponential backoff starting at 250 ms.
- Firefox 141 users may see a flicker during widget hydration; a fix
  is planned for v3.2.1.
