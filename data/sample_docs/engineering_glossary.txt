ENGINEERING GLOSSARY

Adaptive Router
   A system component that decides at request time which downstream model
   or service should handle a given request, typically based on cost,
   latency, and quality trade-offs.

Cold Start
   The additional latency incurred when a service or model is invoked
   after a period of inactivity, due to initialization overhead
   (loading weights, warming caches, spinning up containers).

Load Fraction
   The ratio of in-flight requests to the maximum concurrent capacity of
   a service. A load fraction of 1.0 means the service is fully saturated.

p95 Latency
   The 95th percentile of a latency distribution: 95% of requests
   complete faster than this value. A common tail-latency SLO metric.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
   A pattern where an LLM's answer is grounded in documents retrieved
   from an external knowledge base, typically via vector similarity
   search over embedded text chunks.
