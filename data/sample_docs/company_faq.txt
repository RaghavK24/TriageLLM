FROBOZZ TECHNICAL SERVICES — INTERNAL FAQ

Q: What are our support hours?
A: 24/7 for P1 incidents. P2 and P3 tickets are handled 09:00–18:00 UTC
   Monday through Friday. On-call rotation is documented in the runbook.

Q: How do employees request time off?
A: Submit a leave request via the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.
   For urgent leave, contact your manager directly and file the request
   retroactively within 48 hours.

Q: What is the expense reimbursement policy?
A: Employees may expense up to USD 75 per meal while traveling on company
   business. Anything above USD 200 requires manager pre-approval. Receipts
   must be uploaded within 14 days.

Q: What is our security incident escalation policy?
A: Any suspected breach must be reported to the on-call security lead
   within 30 minutes of discovery. A P1 incident ticket must be opened
   immediately. Do not remediate before security has captured forensic data.
